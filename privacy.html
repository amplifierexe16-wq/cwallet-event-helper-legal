Privacy Policy

Cwallet Event Helper | Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Cwallet Event Helper is a Discord bot designed to support community events, reward management, leaderboards and interactive games. This policy explains what information the bot processes and how it is handled.

2. Information We Collect

The bot may store or process the following information:

  • Discord user IDs to identify members.
  • Discord server IDs to separate server data.
  • Santa Hat and Gem balances.
  • Reward and transaction records, including amounts and the users involved in a transaction.
  • Role and permission information needed to verify staff and Legacy member access.

The bot also processes message content when users send bot commands or enter number guesses during an active Guess the Number game.

3. How We Use Information

  • Maintain accurate Santa Hat and Gem balances.
  • Display member balances and leaderboards.
  • Record reward distributions and transfers.
  • Verify staff and Legacy permissions.
  • Operate community games and announce winners.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the bot.

4. Message Content

Message content is processed to recognize bot commands and handle number guesses in active games. The bot is not designed to archive ordinary conversations or use message content to train AI or machine learning models.

5. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Bot-related records are stored in an SQLite database hosted through Railway. Discord provides the platform through which the bot interacts with server members. These services process information as necessary to operate the bot.

We do not sell users' personal information.

6. Data Retention

Reward balances and transaction records may be retained for as long as they are needed to operate the event management system, maintain account balances and preserve transaction history.

Data deletion requests can be submitted to the Cwallet Event Helper administration team.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may contact the administration team through the official Cwallet Discord server to request access to or deletion of their bot-related data.

Requests will be reviewed by the administration team. Some records may need to be retained where necessary for legitimate operational or security purposes.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored bot data and restrict access to authorized administrators. No online storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the Cwallet Event Helper administration team through the official Cwallet Discord server.
